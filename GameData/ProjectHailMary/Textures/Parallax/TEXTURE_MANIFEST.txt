Project Hail Mary — Parallax Texture Manifest
=============================================

All textures go in this folder: GameData/ProjectHailMary/Textures/Parallax/

EXPORT SETTINGS (Substance Painter / Photoshop / GIMP):
  Format:      DDS
  Compression: BC3/DXT5 for normal maps (linear, no sRGB)
               BC1/DXT1 for albedo maps (sRGB)
  Resolution:  2048×2048 recommended (1024×1024 minimum)
  Mipmaps:     YES (generate in DDS export)
  Normal maps: DirectX convention (Y / green channel NOT flipped)
